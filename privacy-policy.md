Home Inventory helps you document the contents of your home (rooms, items,
photos, receipts and reports), for example for insurance. This policy explains
what information the app collects, where it goes, and the choices you have.

## Who can use the app

The app is for people **13 years of age or older**. When you create an account
you enter your date of birth and the app will not let you continue if you are
under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under
13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we
will delete it.

## Information we collect

### 1. Account sign-up information (stored by us)

When you create an account you give us:

- **Required:** Home Country, email address, and date of birth.
- **Optional:** first and last name, phone number, state/province, gender,
  marital status, household income range, and education level.

We use your date of birth **once**, on your device, to check that you are old
enough. **We do not store your full date of birth** — we keep only your birth
year and a record that you passed the age check.

This information is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). We use it to
understand who is using the app and how, and to contact you about the app. We
do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

### 2. Your inventory data (stored in your own Google account)

Your properties, rooms, items, item details, photos, documents and generated
reports are saved in **your own Google Drive and Google Sheets**, in files and
folders the app creates for you. We ask Google only for the "drive.file"
permission, which lets the app see and change **only the files it created** — not
the rest of your Drive. We do not receive a copy of this data; it stays between
your device and your Google account. You can view, move or delete these files in
Google Drive at any time.

### 3. Usage and crash information (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to see which screens of the app are used, and
Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash and error reports so we can fix problems.
These services may collect device and app information such as device model,
operating system version, app version, an app-instance identifier, and the
technical details of a crash.

### 4. Photos and camera

The app asks for permission to use your camera and photo library so you can
photograph rooms and items and attach existing pictures. Photos are saved to your
Google Drive (see above). The app can read text from a photo (for example a
serial number) **on your device**; the photo is not sent anywhere for this.

### 5. Address suggestions

When you type your home address, the text you type is sent to a map service to
suggest matching addresses: **Apple Maps** on iPhone and iPad, and the **Google
Places API** on Android. Those services receive what you type, and their own
privacy policies apply. We do not receive your location from your device, and the
app does not use GPS.

### 6. Bug reports and feedback

When you use "Report a Problem" or "Suggest an Improvement", the app opens a
draft email in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you send it, and you can
edit it first.

## How we use information

- To provide and improve the app and fix problems.
- To understand who uses the app and how (summaries of sign-up answers and
  screen usage).
- To enforce the minimum age.
- To respond when you contact us.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for
advertising.

## Who we share information with

We use service providers to run the app: **Google** (Firebase, Google sign-in,
Google Drive/Sheets, Google Places on Android) and **Apple** (App Store, Apple
Maps address suggestions on iPhone/iPad). They process data for us under their
own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if the law
requires it.

## Where information is processed

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other
countries where they operate.

## How long we keep information

We keep sign-up information for as long as we need it for the purposes above or
until you ask us to delete it. Your inventory data stays in your Google account
until you delete it there.

## Your choices and rights

- **Access, correction or deletion:** email sales@renegadesupplies.com and we
  will act on requests about your sign-up information. Depending on where you
  live (for example California, or the European Economic Area and United
  Kingdom), you may have additional legal rights, which we will honor.
- **Your inventory data:** manage or delete it directly in Google Drive.
- **Google sign-in:** you can revoke the app's access at any time from your
  Google Account's security settings.
- **Permissions:** you can turn off camera and photo access in your phone's
  settings; some features will then stop working.

## Security

Sign-up records can be created by the app but cannot be read, changed or deleted
by other apps or users, and your inventory lives in your own Google account,
protected by your Google sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot
guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. If a
change is significant we will tell you in the app.
